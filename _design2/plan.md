## Risks
- CLOTH PIN HANDSHAKE is the riskiest seam. model.position is set from alpha-interpolated body pos while joints animate off raw dt; the design's instinct to keep ALL cloth math in ROOT-LOCAL space (waistRing relative to root) is what makes this consistent — DO NOT mix world body pos into particle space. The spec self-contradicts on pinning (overwrite prev vs not): LOCKED to prev[pinned]=previous-ring for velocity transfer. If trailing looks dead or twitchy, this is the line to check first. Mitigation: the lathe-on-waistRing fallback is already wired from STEP 3, so cloth can be disabled without touching figure code.
- FRAMERATE-DEPENDENT BEHAVIOR was the dominant flaw across cloth AND figure. Cloth: the fixed-step 1/60 accumulator is MANDATORY (not the spec's 'optional') — per-render-frame Verlet ships broken on 120/144Hz laptops. Figure: every creepFX/lerp must use 1-exp(-k*dt). If either ships with raw-frame lerps it looks fine on the 60Hz dev box and broken everywhere else — the failure mode is invisible in dev.
- COLOR GRADE IS IN LINEAR SPACE (pass runs before OutputPass/ACES). The locked lift/tint numbers are doubled/reduced guesses; they WILL need empirical retune after the first screenshot or the look muddies to grey when ACES rolls off the lifted blacks. Treat config.js post values as starting points, not final. Instrument max channel via window.GDBG if needed.
- FOG MODEL: the original 0.16 recommendation used the wrong (linear) fog model; FogExp2 is exp(-(d*x)^2). Locked to 0.11 base / 0.13 cap. If a future tuner bumps density to 'feel oppressive', they will re-hide granny at her 8-12m approach range — oppression must come from grade+fixtures, NOT density. Guard with the hard cap.
- AUDIO LOW-END / LIMITER: the master limiter is brutal (thr -3, ratio 20, rel 0.1). Cutting the dread sub-drone + adding a local compressor on the dread bus is what prevents pumping on every heartbeat. If the sub-drone is re-added later 'for more dread', expect audible pumping — it overlaps existing 42Hz/55Hz beds anyway.
- STROBE / ACCESSIBILITY: with dread latched at 1.0 during long chases, even the cut-down flashlight-only failure can be intense. The reduceFlicker settings flag MUST be wired into the build (not left in 'risks' like the original) — it gates all hard dropouts. Default false, but present.
- VOID-EYE LIGHTING: MeshBasicMaterial 0x000000 is the ONLY reliable 'hole' under the permanent hemi+ambient (MeshStandard 0x000000 still receives ambient and reads as grey paint). Verify with flashlight OFF specifically — that is when MeshStandard sockets would betray themselves.
- SCREENSHOT VERIFICATION needs manual frame driving: the preview runs the page hidden so rAF/visibilitychange suspends audio + the loop. Use window.GDBG.manualFrame(dt) (already exposed) to step, and force G.state='playing' + positions via GDBG before each screenshot.

## Acceptance checks
- STEP 0: Game boots, granny renders identical to before, no console errors. Screenshot via preview_start + preview_screenshot at menu->play; compare granny silhouette to baseline (unchanged).
- VISUALS calm: with granny far (force GDBG.G.granny.distToPlayer large, awareness 0), screenshot shows mild desaturation, slow breathing vignette, grain visibly shimmering frame-to-frame (step manualFrame twice, diff the two PNGs — grain pixels must differ; if identical the seed bug regressed).
- VISUALS dread: force chasing=true (GDBG.G.granny.state='chase') and step a few frames; screenshot shows heavier olive/sepia grade, tighter darker vignette, dimmer sagged fixtures. Granny at 8m must still be a READABLE silhouette through fog (NOT a grey wall) — this is the fog-cap acceptance.
- FLASHLIGHT failure: over ~30 stepped frames at dread=1, capture a sequence; at least one frame shows the flashlight near-black (dropout floor 0.04) with the shadow still present (not popped). With reduceFlicker=true, NO dropout frames appear.
- FOG horizon: screenshot looking at a far wall/opening — no hard seam between fogged geometry and background (background.copy verified).
- FIGURE eyes (flashlight OFF): GDBG.core.setFlashlight(false), face granny, screenshot — eye sockets read as black HOLES (not grey), with two small bright glints. With flashlight ON, holes remain dark, glints catch the beam.
- FIGURE stillness: set state='chase', moveSpeed 0; over several stepped frames the body is nearly frozen (freeze-stare), legs/arms not shuffling. Then set moveSpeed 1.4 — gait resumes. Confirm head doesn't drift over 100 stepped frames (absolute assignment, no += creep).
- FIGURE lunge: set lungeT ramping 0->0.35 across frames; screenshots show rear-back then forward thrust (two-phase), upper leaning from ~0.22 to ~1.07 rad.
- CLOTH drape (desktop): granny standing still; screenshot shows a conical dress draped to mid-calf, no visible vertical seam where the cylinder closes, no leg poke-through, lit correctly inside and out (no black interior).
- CLOTH trailing: step frames while moveSpeed>0 and during a lunge; the hem lags/swings behind the waist (compare hem position across 3 frames — it must trail, not move rigidly with the body).
- CLOTH respawn: call GDBG.G.granny.reset() then step one frame and screenshot — dress is immediately draped at the new position with NO streak across the room (cloth.reset verified).
- CLOTH framerate: step with dt=1/144 for 60 frames then dt=1/40 for 60 frames; the rest drape height/stiffness must look the same (fixed-step accumulator verified) — capture both and compare hem Y.
- AUDIO (manual, no screenshot): in a real (non-preview) run, as granny approaches, radio static rises in gain+brightness; metallic groans get more frequent; on chase-start a dreadDrop vacuum fires; on patrol->investigate a whisper plays once. Confirm via GDBG.AudioEngine state + ear; confirm no audible pumping on heartbeats and jumpscare still has its silence (dreadDrop suppressed within 2s of jumpscare).
- MOBILE: set GDBG.MOBILE path (or UA spoof) — granny uses lathe (GDBG.G.granny.cloth===null), no cloth arrays allocated; grade is milder; no flashlight dropouts; audio quality 'low' (static only). Screenshot confirms rigid skirt swaying with the body via waistRing.
