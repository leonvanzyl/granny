GRANNY

FIVE DAYS TO GET OUT
Seed:
You wake locked inside her house. You have 5 days. Find the keys, tools and codes hidden across the rooms, unlock the three locks on the front door, and run. She hunts by sound and sight — move slow to stay quiet, hide when she's close, and never let her corner you.

HOW TO SURVIVE

WASD — move  ·  Mouse — look  ·  Shift — sprint (loud, costs stamina)

Ctrl / C — crouch-sneak (near silent)  ·  E — interact / pick up / open

F — flashlight  ·  Q — drop held item  ·  Right-click — throw held item (lure her to the noise)

G — hide (at a wardrobe / under a bed)  ·  Space (held) — hold breath while hiding

Listen: her footsteps are muffled through walls and sharp in the open. The heartbeat means she's close. Push furniture against doors to slow her — but shoving is loud. A catch costs a day, not your progress.
