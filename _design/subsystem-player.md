# SUBSYSTEM: player

## Summary
The player is a KINEMATIC capsule in cannon-es: collisionResponse driven by hand-rolled "collide-and-slide" against the world so the player is solidly stopped by walls but can still PUSH dynamic furniture by transferring velocity on contact (a pure dynamic body for an FPS produces jitter, sliding-down-slopes drift, and stair-snag, so it is rejected). The capsule is r=0.30 m, standing total height 1.80 m (eye at 1.62 m), crouch total height 1.00 m (eye at 0.85 m). Three speed states: crouch-sneak 1.2 m/s, walk 2.8 m/s, sprint 5.2 m/s, with separate accel (18/14/12 m/s²) and decel (22 m/s²) so motion feels weighty but responsive. Mouse-look is yaw on a player Object3D + pitch on the camera child, pitch clamped to ±88°, sensitivity 0.0022 rad/px, accumulated from raw movementX/Y in pointerlockchange-gated handlers. Stamina is 0..100, sprint drains 18/s, regen 12/s after a 1.2 s delay, locks sprint until ≥20 when emptied. The NOISE EMISSION MODEL is the controller's contract with the AI: discrete NoiseEvent {position, loudness 0..1, radius m} emitted on footstep cadence (cadence scales with speed) and on impulse events (door slam, dropped item, bump, landing). Footstep radius: sneak 1.5 m / walk 7 m / sprint 16 m; impulses spike to 6–22 m. Interaction is a 2.4 m forward raycast against an "interactables" layer with hover-highlight (emissive rim) + context prompt. Held items track a smoothed view-space socket and are released as real dynamic bodies (throw = inherit camera-forward velocity). HIDING reparents the camera into a fixed in-prop framing transform, suppresses footstep noise entirely, removes the player from Granny's vision set UNLESS she has line-of-sight to the hide spot or saw the entry within a memory window; hold-breath reduces a passive breathing-noise tick but drains a breath meter that, when empty, forces a loud gasp NoiseEvent.

## Key Decisions
- KINEMATIC capsule, not dynamic. The controller integrates velocity manually and resolves world collisions via collide-and-slide; furniture pushing is done by applying impulses to dynamic bodies on contact rather than relying on the solver. This is the single most important decision and the source of every 'it jitters / sinks / catches on stairs' failure when done wrong.
- Yaw and pitch are split across two transforms: yaw rotates the player root (which the capsule and movement basis follow), pitch rotates ONLY the camera. This prevents the classic bug where pitching tilts your movement direction or rolls the world.
- Three discrete speed STATES with a single resolved target speed per frame, not additive modifiers. State priority: crouch overrides sprint (you cannot sprint while crouched), sprint requires stamina and forward intent.
- Noise is an explicit event stream the AI subscribes to, decoupled from the audio that plays it. The same NoiseEvent both (a) triggers a procedural Web Audio one-shot and (b) is pushed to the AI hearing queue. Never let the AI 'read the audio' — it reads events.
- Crouch height transition is animated (capsule shrinks/grows over 0.18 s) with an overhead clearance check before standing, so you can't stand up inside a low cabinet and clip through it.
- Hiding is a controller STATE that hard-overrides movement input and owns the camera; it talks to the AI via an explicit 'concealed' flag plus a 'lastSeenEntering' timestamp the AI checks, rather than the AI magically knowing.

## Data Model
PlayerController owns: body (CANNON.Body, type KINEMATIC, shape Sphere-capped capsule approximated as 1 Sphere + cylinder via a compound of 2 Spheres at top/bottom + we use a custom sweep, see algorithms); position Vec3, velocityH (horizontal Vec3), velocityY (number); yaw (rad), pitch (rad); state enum {STAND, CROUCH}; moving enum {IDLE, SNEAK, WALK, SPRINT}; targetSpeed, currentSpeedScalar; isGrounded bool, groundNormal Vec3, coyoteTimer, lastLandImpactSpeed; capsuleHalfHeight (animated 0.90→0.50), eyeHeight (animated 1.62→0.85), crouchT (0..1 lerp param); stamina (0..100), staminaRegenDelayTimer, sprintLocked bool; bobPhase (rad), bobAmp, bobFreq; stepDistanceAccumulator (m) for footstep cadence; heldItem (ref or null), heldItemSocketCurrent Vec3/Quat (smoothed); hoverTarget (ref or null), lastPromptText; hiding {active bool, spot ref, exitTransform, breath (0..100), breathHolding bool, enteredAtTime}. Emits NoiseEvent {position:Vec3, loudness:0..1, radius:m, type:string, time}. Reads InputState {keys set, mouseDX, mouseDY, interactPressed, crouchHeld/Toggled, sprintHeld, dropPressed, breathHeld}.

## Public Interface
- new PlayerController(world, camera, audioBus, aiSensory, spawnPos) — constructs kinematic body, wires camera as child of yaw root.
- update(dt) — main per-frame tick: reads input, resolves state, integrates+slides, animates crouch/bob, updates stamina, fires footstep/impulse NoiseEvents, updates held item & hover, services hiding. dt clamped to <=1/30 to prevent tunneling on frame spikes.
- getEyeWorldPosition() : Vec3 and getLookDirection() : Vec3 — consumed by AI vision (to test the cone origin) and by interaction raycast.
- emitNoise(type, loudnessOverride?) : NoiseEvent — central choke point; pushes to aiSensory.hear(event) and audioBus.playNoise(event). All noise routes through here.
- isConcealed() : bool and getConcealmentInfo() : {concealed, spot, enteredAtTime} — AI calls this; vision module skips the player when concealed unless it has LOS to the spot or now-enteredAtTime < graceWindow.
- pickUp(item) / dropHeld(throw:bool) — transfers an item to/from the view socket; drop re-enables the dynamic body and (if throw) sets its velocity to lookDir*throwSpeed and fires a 'drop' noise on next impact.
- enterHide(spot) / exitHide() — swaps to hiding state, animates camera into/out of the prop framing transform over 0.35 s.
- setSensitivity(radPerPx), applyConfig(cfg) — runtime tuning hooks.
- onLanded(impactSpeed) — internal callback from ground detection that converts impact speed to a landing NoiseEvent and optional stumble.

## Algorithms
- PER-FRAME ORDER (update(dt)): 1) clamp dt; 2) consume mouse deltas -> yaw/pitch; 3) resolve movement state; 4) compute desired horizontal velocity & accelerate/decelerate; 5) apply gravity to velocityY; 6) integrate position via collide-and-slide; 7) ground detection & landing; 8) animate crouch height & eye; 9) head-bob; 10) stamina; 11) footstep/impulse noise; 12) held item tracking; 13) interaction raycast & hover; 14) hiding service. Order matters: collision before ground-check before bob before noise (noise needs grounded+step distance).
- MOUSE-LOOK: in pointerlock handler accumulate raw e.movementX/Y. In update: yaw -= dx*sens; pitch -= dy*sens; pitch = clamp(pitch, -88°,+88°). Apply yaw to player root quaternion (Y axis only), pitch to camera quaternion (X axis only). NEVER write both to one object's Euler — that introduces roll.
- STATE RESOLUTION: if crouchActive -> state=CROUCH, candidate=SNEAK. else if sprintHeld && stamina>0 && !sprintLocked && forwardInput>0 -> SPRINT. else if any move key -> WALK. else IDLE. targetSpeed = map(state). Crouch blocks sprint by construction.
- MOVEMENT INTEGRATION: build wishDir from WASD in yaw-basis (normalize so diagonal isn't faster). targetVel = wishDir*targetSpeed. a = (input present? accel(state) : decel) * (grounded?1:airControlFactor). velocityH = moveTowards(velocityH, targetVel, a*dt). This gives weighty start/stop. Do NOT just set velocity=wishDir*speed (causes ice-skating instant stops the client will reject).
- COLLIDE-AND-SLIDE (the core): treat player as a capsule. Each frame, decompose desired displacement = (velocityH + (0,velocityY,0))*dt. Iterate up to 4 times: ray/shape-sweep the capsule along remaining motion against static world bodies; on hit at fraction t, advance by t, then subtract the into-surface component (project remaining onto the contact plane: remaining -= n*(remaining·n)); repeat with leftover. This stops at walls, slides along them, climbs small steps if a step-up probe (cast +0.3 m up, forward, down) succeeds. Manually move kinematic body.position to result; set body.velocity so cannon reports contacts to dynamic furniture.
- PUSHING FURNITURE: because the player is kinematic it won't be stopped by furniture in the solver; instead, in the world's contact events, for each contact between player and a DYNAMIC body, apply an impulse to the furniture = clamp(playerSpeed)*pushMass factor along the contact normal, and also block the player's own slide by including dynamic bodies' broadphase AABBs in the sweep when their mass is high (heavy wardrobe stops you; light chair gets shoved). Threshold: bodies with mass>40 kg are treated as walls for the player sweep; lighter ones are pushable and do not block.
- GROUND DETECTION: short downward sphere-cast (radius=capsuleRadius-0.02, length=groundSnapDistance+skin). If hit & normal.y>0.6 -> grounded, snap to surface, store groundNormal; reset coyoteTimer. If was airborne and now grounded, onLanded(prevVelocityY magnitude). Coyote: remain 'grounded for jump/step' for 0.10 s after leaving ground.
- CROUCH TRANSITION: crouchT moves toward (crouchActive?1:0) at dt/crouchTransitionTime. halfHeight = lerp(standHalf, crouchHalf, smoothstep(crouchT)); eyeHeight likewise; resize capsule shape & keep FEET planted (raise center, not feet, so you don't sink). STAND-UP GUARD: before allowing crouchActive->false, sphere-cast upward from current head to standHeight; if blocked, force stay crouched. Prevents clipping through low geometry.
- HEAD-BOB: only when grounded & moving. bobPhase += 2π*freq(state)*dt. vertical = sin(bobPhase)*amp(state); lateral = sin(bobPhase*0.5)*amp*bobLateralRatio. Apply as a small offset to camera local position ON TOP of eyeHeight; ease amp toward 0 when idle (lerp 8/s) so it doesn't snap. A footstep noise fires at each bob bottom (sin crosses downward zero) as a cheap cadence source for STAND walk/sprint.
- FOOTSTEP CADENCE & NOISE: accumulate stepDistanceAccumulator += horizontalSpeed*dt while grounded&moving. When it exceeds stride(state), reset and emitNoise('footstep') with loudness/radius for current moving-state. This makes faster movement fire more frequent + louder noise automatically and ties noise to actual distance traveled (not wall-clock), so being blocked by a wall while holding W emits NO footsteps. Crouch-sneak uses tiny radius (1.5 m) so it's near-silent.
- STAMINA: if SPRINT this frame: stamina -= drain*dt; regenDelayTimer=regenDelay. else: regenDelayTimer-=dt; if<=0 stamina += regen*dt. clamp 0..100. if stamina hits 0 -> sprintLocked=true; clear when stamina>=20. Sprint request while locked falls back to WALK.
- LANDING: onLanded(impactSpeed): if impactSpeed<landSoft -> nothing. else loudness=remap(impactSpeed, soft..hard ->0.3..1.0), radius=remap(...8..22). emitNoise('land'). if impactSpeed>landHard -> brief stumble: clamp horizontal speed to walk for 0.4 s + extra camera bob dip.
- INTERACTION RAYCAST: from eye along lookDir, length 2.4 m, against interactables layer. Hover: if hit & item is reachable (a reach check: hit point within 2.4 m AND, if standing-only item, player not forced-crouched) -> set emissive rim highlight + prompt ('[E] Pick up Key', '[E] Open', '[F] Hide'). On interactPressed dispatch to the hit object's interact(). Clear highlight when ray misses.
- HELD ITEM TRACK: target world transform = camera.matrix * heldSocketOffset (+ tiny sway from velocity & look-delta for life). heldItemSocketCurrent = expLerp(current, target, 1-exp(-heldSmoothing*dt)). The item's mesh follows this; its physics body is set to type=KINEMATIC (no collisions) while held so it never shoves the player or jitters. On drop: set body dynamic, place at socket, velocity = throw? lookDir*throwSpeed : carryVelocity; arm a one-shot 'drop' NoiseEvent that fires on its first significant collision (impulse>threshold).
- HIDING: enterHide(spot): set hiding.active, store exitTransform=current camera world, disable movement input, blend camera over 0.35 s into spot.viewTransform (e.g. slats of wardrobe / dark under-bed framing), zero velocity, set body to a tucked-away position/disable its sweep. Footstep & bump noise suppressed entirely. breathHeld: if held, breath -= drain*dt and passive breathing-noise tick is muted; if breath<=0 -> force release + emitNoise('gasp', 0.85, 14 m) (this is how a careless player holding breath too long gets caught). If !breathHeld, a soft passive breathing noise tick (loudness 0.08, radius 2 m) fires every ~2 s. AI INTEGRATION: while concealed, vision module excludes player UNLESS (a) AI has LOS to spot.lookoutPoint, or (b) now - hiding.enteredAtTime < hideEntryGraceWindow (6 s) AND she was in a state where she could have seen the entry (her own vision check at entry time set sawEntry=true). If sawEntry, she walks to the spot and 'checks' it (opens wardrobe) -> forced exitHide + caught. exitHide(): reverse blend, restore body, re-enable input.

## Pitfalls
- Using a DYNAMIC rigid body for the player. Result: jitter against walls, sliding down ramps, catching on tiny floor seams, spinning. FIX: kinematic capsule + manual collide-and-slide; only furniture is dynamic.
- Tunneling through thin walls/floors at high speed or on a frame hitch. FIX: clamp dt to 1/30, and SWEEP the capsule (shape-cast) along the displacement instead of teleport-then-check; never move more than ~radius per sub-step (split displacement into ceil(len/(radius*0.8)) sub-steps).
- Diagonal movement faster than straight (W+D = 1.41x). FIX: normalize wishDir before scaling by speed.
- Pitch applied to the same object as yaw, or movement basis taken from the camera (which is pitched). Result: walking 'into the floor' when looking down, world roll. FIX: yaw on root, pitch on camera only; build wishDir from yaw-only basis.
- Footsteps fired on a wall-clock timer. Result: you emit noise while standing still pushed against a wall holding W, or footsteps continue at constant rate regardless of speed. FIX: distance-accumulator cadence gated on actual horizontal displacement while grounded.
- Crouch shrinks the capsule from the center so feet lift off the floor (you float) or grows from center into the ceiling (you clip up through it). FIX: keep feet planted (anchor bottom), and overhead clearance sphere-cast before standing.
- Items parented to camera with full physics still enabled -> they collide with the player capsule and the world, causing violent jitter or pushing the player. FIX: held item body becomes kinematic/no-collision; only smoothed visual tracking; re-enable dynamics on drop.
- Item placement spawns floating or inside furniture (this is a HARD client requirement). The controller's drop must validate: sphere-cast down to nearest surface and settle the body there; reject drop positions that overlap solids (nudge to nearest free cell). Coordinate with the placement subsystem's collision-validated grid.
- AI 'reads the audio output' or polls player.speed to hear. Result: tight coupling, inaudible bugs, hearing through walls wrong. FIX: single emitNoise() choke point producing explicit NoiseEvents with position+radius; AI does its own distance/occlusion test on the event.
- Hiding makes the player permanently invisible to Granny -> trivializes the game. FIX: enteredAtTime grace window + LOS-to-spot check + sawEntry flag so she pulls you out if she watched you hide.
- Hold-breath with no cost -> infinite safe hiding. FIX: breath meter that forces a loud gasp NoiseEvent when depleted.
- Stamina that lets you spam sprint by tapping. FIX: regenDelay before regen starts, and sprintLocked until stamina>=20 after empty.
- Head-bob writing absolute camera Y (fighting crouch/eye-height animation) or not easing to zero -> camera snaps on stop. FIX: bob is an additive offset on top of eyeHeight, amplitude eased to 0 when idle.
- movementX/Y read outside pointer-lock (huge jumps when lock lost/regained). FIX: gate accumulation on document.pointerLockElement === canvas; discard the first event after (re)lock.

## Risks
- cannon-es has no built-in capsule shape or shape-sweep; collide-and-slide must be implemented with manual raycasts/sphere-casts against the static set, or by approximating the capsule as 2 spheres. This is the highest-effort, highest-risk part — budget time for it and unit-test against a step, a thin wall, a slope, and a corner.
- Kinematic-vs-dynamic furniture pushing (impulse on contact + mass-threshold blocking) can feel inconsistent at the boundary; needs tuning so heavy items reliably stop you and light items reliably shove without you passing through them.
- Distance-based footstep cadence must use the SAME grounded/displacement values the collide-and-slide produced (post-collision actual movement), or a wall-blocked player still emits noise; ensure the accumulator reads resolved displacement, not desired.
- Hiding camera reparenting can introduce a one-frame pop or fight the bob/crouch systems; those systems must be disabled while hiding.active and the camera must be cleanly restored on exit (store and reapply exact pre-hide transform).
- The hideEntryGraceWindow + sawEntry logic depends on the AI vision module being queried AT entry time; if the AI tick and controller tick race, sawEntry can be missed — define a deterministic order (controller calls aiSensory.notifyHideEntry(spot, eyePos) synchronously in enterHide).
- dt clamp at 1/30 means on a sustained <30 fps machine the game runs in slow-motion rather than tunneling; acceptable trade but should be noted, and a max sub-step count caps worst-case cost.

## CRITIQUE (worksProbability=0.28)
Verdict: Architecturally this is the strongest of the failure-prone subsystems — the big-rock decisions (kinematic capsule + manual collide-and-slide, split yaw/pitch transforms, distance-accumulator footsteps, noise-as-event-stream, stand-up clearance check, breath/gasp cost on hiding) are CORRECT and are exactly what separates a working FPS controller from the jittery garbage the client has rejected before. However, the design is hand-waved at precisely the two places that consume 70% of the implementation effort and 90% of the bugs: (1) the collide-and-slide sweep itself, which is described as if cannon-es provides shape-casting (it does not — and the fallback "approximate capsule as 2 spheres" is mentioned but never specified), and (2) the kinematic-vs-dynamic furniture interaction, which as written is internally contradictory and will either let you walk through chairs or get you stuck on them. Several numeric values are also miscalibrated for fun/tension. As written, a careful implementer still ships something that catches on corners, pushes furniture inconsistently, and has a hiding mechanic that is either trivial or unfair. It will NOT work on first integrated build. The fixes are concrete and known, but they must be spelled out far beyond what this doc does.

### Fatal Flaws
- COLLIDE-AND-SLIDE IS HAND-WAVED ON THE ONE THING THAT MATTERS: cannon-es has NO shape-cast / capsule-sweep API. It has Ray (raycast against bodies) and that is essentially it. The design says 'ray/shape-sweep the capsule along remaining motion' as if sweepConvex exists. It does not. You cannot sweep a capsule in cannon-es. So the entire core algorithm is built on a primitive that the chosen engine does not provide. Result on integrated build: implementer either (a) does a handful of point raycasts from the capsule axis and gets corner/edge tunneling and snagging, or (b) tries to use the narrowphase manually and discovers cannon-es does not expose a clean 'overlap query + MTV' for arbitrary shape pairs either. This is THE risk the doc flags as 'highest' but then does not actually solve.
- FURNITURE PUSHING MODEL IS SELF-CONTRADICTORY AND WILL NOT WORK AS DESCRIBED: The body is KINEMATIC with manual collide-and-slide against the STATIC set only. Then pushing is done two incompatible ways at once: (a) 'apply an impulse in the world's contact events for player-vs-dynamic contacts' and (b) 'include dynamic bodies' AABBs in the sweep when mass>40kg'. Problem: a KINEMATIC body in cannon-es DOES generate contacts with dynamic bodies and WILL shove them via the solver automatically (kinematic bodies have effectively infinite mass) — so manually applying an additional impulse double-counts and launches light furniture across the room. Meanwhile the collide-and-slide sweep is described as testing the STATIC set, so to make heavy furniture block you, you must inject specific dynamic bodies into your own hand-rolled sweep — but your sweep is raycast-based and dynamic bodies move, so the mass>40 threshold creates a hard pop the instant a wardrobe's mass crosses the line. Net: the boundary behavior the doc itself flags as a risk is not just untuned, it is architecturally guaranteed to be wrong because two collision systems (cannon solver + your manual sweep) both act on the same contact.
- KINEMATIC BODY + MANUAL position WRITE BREAKS cannon-es CONTACT/VELOCITY REPORTING UNLESS done a very specific way: the doc says 'Manually move kinematic body.position to result; set body.velocity so cannon reports contacts to dynamic furniture.' For a KINEMATIC body, cannon-es derives the contact-resolution velocity from body.velocity, NOT from the position delta — and if you teleport body.position every frame AND also set body.velocity, the two disagree (position moved by collide-and-slide which already removed into-wall components, but velocity is your pre-slide intent). The solver will use the velocity to push furniture in directions you already slid away from, causing furniture to get knocked even when you slid along a wall and never actually moved toward it. Correct approach (unstated): set body.velocity = actualResolvedDisplacement/dt and let cannon integrate the kinematic body itself for that step, OR keep the player body collisionFilter-excluded from furniture entirely and do ALL pushing manually. The doc tries to do both and they fight.
- NO JUMP + gravity -18 + groundSnap 0.25 + step-up probe is an over-engineered air/ground system for a game that has no verticality. With jumpVelocity=0 and a single-floor mansion, coyoteTime, maxFallSpeed -28, air-control 0.30, and landing-impact noise from falls will essentially never trigger in normal play (you only fall when... there are no stairs to fall off described). Either the level has real drops (then 0.25m groundSnap will make you stutter down every step edge and the 'climb small steps via +0.3m probe' will fight groundSnap), or it does not (then half this system is dead code that still has to be debugged). This is effort spent where there is no fun, and the step-up probe vs ground-snap interaction is a classic source of the exact 'stair-snag' the doc claims to prevent.

### Concrete Fixes
- REPLACE the fictional capsule sweep with a concrete, cannon-es-real algorithm: model the player as a vertical segment of N=3 spheres (centers at feet+r, mid, head-r; r=0.30) and resolve collisions by ITERATIVE DEPENETRATION against static convex/box/trimesh world bodies using cannon's narrowphase OR, more reliably, by your own analytic sphere-vs-AABB/sphere-vs-triangle tests since the world is procedurally generated (you KNOW every wall is an axis-aligned box — exploit that). Per substep: (1) split desired displacement into ceil(len/(r*0.5))=ceil(len/0.15m) substeps so max move/substep ≤0.15m (half-radius, not 0.8r — 0.8r=0.24m still tunnels a 0.20m gap); (2) advance one substep; (3) for each of the 3 spheres find deepest penetration against nearby static AABBs (broadphase: query a 1m-padded AABB), push out along the contact normal by penetration depth, and project residual velocity: vH -= n*(vH·n); (4) repeat depenetration up to 4 iterations to settle corners. This needs zero shape-cast and is deterministic and unit-testable. Budget 2-3 days; it is the load-bearing wall of the whole game.
- FIX furniture pushing by picking ONE owner of the interaction, not both. Recommended: put the player kinematic body in its OWN collision group that does NOT collide with furniture in the solver (collisionFilterGroup/Mask), and do ALL pushing manually: each frame, for any dynamic body whose AABB overlaps a player sphere, compute relative approach speed along contact normal; if body.mass ≤ 40kg apply impulse J = body.mass * clamp(approachSpeed,0,3.0) along normal (so a 5kg chair gets a believable shove, capped) AND let the player continue (do not block); if body.mass > 40kg, treat that body's current AABB as a static collider in step (3) above for THIS frame so it stops you, and apply zero impulse. Remove the 'set body.velocity so cannon reports contacts' line entirely — the solver is no longer involved in furniture-vs-player. This eliminates the double-count and the pop. Add hysteresis on the mass threshold (block if mass>40, release if mass<35) so a wardrobe whose mass is being modified (e.g. mid-barricade) doesn't flicker.
- SET body.velocity correctly for the kinematic body that DOES still need to exist for AI/spatial queries: after collide-and-slide produces resolvedDisplacement, set body.position = result AND body.velocity.set(resolvedDisp.x/dt, resolvedDisp.y/dt, resolvedDisp.z/dt) so any system reading body.velocity (e.g. a doppler audio cue) gets the TRUE post-collision velocity, not intent. Do this AFTER collision, never before.
- RECALIBRATE speeds for tension. Sprint 5.2 m/s (=18.7 km/h) in a cramped mansion is too fast — you will overshoot doorways and it kills the 'creeping dread' loop; Granny clones live at slow speeds. Suggest: sneak 1.0, walk 2.4, sprint 4.2 m/s. Crucially, specify Granny's speed relative to these (not in this doc, but the player doc MUST state the contract): if Granny ≥ sprint speed the game is unfair, if ≤ walk it is trivial — target Granny ≈ 3.6 m/s so sprint barely outruns her and costs stamina, creating the decision. Drop gravity to -16 (−18 with no jump just makes landings from the only-if-any steps harsher for no benefit) OR keep -18 but then DELETE the air-control/coyote/jump scaffolding to remove dead complexity.
- HEAD-BOB at sprint amp 0.065m vertical + 1.9–3.0Hz will cause motion sickness and, worse, will throw your interaction raycast and AI vision-cone ORIGIN around 6.5cm every step. FIX: (a) cap sprint vertical amp to 0.04m, (b) DECOUPLE the eye position used for gameplay raycasts/AI-cone from the bobbed render camera — getEyeWorldPosition() must return the UN-bobbed, un-swayed eye so interaction/vision is stable; only the render camera gets bob. The doc never says which eye position feeds AI vision; if it feeds the bobbed one, Granny's ability to see you will flicker with your footsteps. Spell this out explicitly.
- TIE footstep accumulator to RESOLVED horizontal displacement length, not horizontalSpeed*dt (the doc says speed*dt in the STAMINA/footstep prose but speed is pre-collision). Compute strideAccum += resolvedHorizDisp.length() each frame. This is the only way the 'blocked against a wall emits no footstep' guarantee actually holds; as written ('horizontalSpeed*dt') it does NOT hold because horizontalSpeed is your intended speed even when fully blocked.
- MAKE the stand-up clearance check and crouch resize match the collision model: when you grow the capsule you add a 3rd/higher sphere — the upward clearance sphere-cast must use radius=capsuleRadius (0.30), not a thin ray, and must be cast from the crouch-head to stand-head MINUS a 0.02 skin to avoid getting stuck permanently crouched under geometry that is exactly stand-height. Also: if blocked, do not just 'stay crouched' — clamp crouchT growth so the camera doesn't lurch; ease it.
- HIDING grace window of 6s flat is both exploitable and unfair depending on timing. FIX: replace fixed 6s with 'she investigates the hide spot if sawEntry==true OR she has LOS to spot.lookoutPoint, and sawEntry decays only when she fully loses awareness (awareness meter ←0) — not on a wall-clock timer.' A flat 6s means a player who hides while Granny stares directly at them is safe at 6.001s, which the client will call broken. Tie it to the awareness meter the SIGHT system already maintains.

### Missing Details
- WHICH eye transform feeds AI vision and the interaction ray — bobbed render camera vs stable gameplay eye. This is a correctness bug waiting to happen and is completely unspecified.
- Granny's movement/speed numbers — the player speeds are meaningless for balance without the predator's speed stated as an explicit contract. The whole 'sprint to escape costs stamina' decision is undefined.
- How the static world is represented for the manual sweep: are walls AABB boxes, trimeshes, or convex hulls? The depenetration math is totally different. Since it's procedural, this should be locked to AABBs/boxes for sane, cheap collision — but the doc never commits.
- Concrete capsule->cannon shape mapping. 'compound of 2 Spheres + custom sweep' is not a shape. Specify: N spheres at fixed local offsets, radius 0.30, and that the cannon body shape is only used (if at all) for furniture-blocking queries, not solver response.
- Door barricading mechanics interaction: the doc says you can push furniture to barricade doors, but never defines how a pushed dynamic body actually impedes a hinge-constrained door (does the door's swing collide with the dynamic body in the solver? does the player body? mass interplay?). This is a headline client requirement and the player controller's pushing model directly determines whether it works.
- Drop/throw validation against the placement grid is named but the actual settle algorithm (sphere-cast down, reject overlap, 'nudge to nearest free cell') has no numbers: cast length, overlap test, what 'free cell' means, and what happens if no free cell exists within range (item must NOT despawn or float — a hard client requirement).
- Frame-order race with the physics step: update(dt) writes body.position, but when does world.step() run relative to update()? If you write position then step, the solver may move furniture using stale player velocity; if you step then write, contacts lag a frame. Must be specified (recommend: update() sets velocities, then world.step(fixedDt) with accumulator, then read-back — and decouple your manual collide-and-slide from cannon's step to avoid two integrators fighting).
- Fixed-timestep handling: dt is clamped to 1/30 but cannon-es needs a FIXED internal timestep with accumulator (world.step(fixed, dt, maxSub)) for stable furniture; the player's variable-dt collide-and-slide and cannon's fixed-step furniture must be reconciled or furniture jitters independently of the (smooth) player.
- Interaction reach for items on tall shelves / inside cabinets: the 'reach check' only tests 2.4m distance and crouch-state, not whether the hit point is inside an openable container that must be opened first — relevant to the 'never hidden inside solid objects' requirement.
- What resets stride/bob/noise state on teleport-like events (enterHide/exitHide, spawn) — failing to reset the accumulator means a footstep noise can fire the frame you exit hiding.
