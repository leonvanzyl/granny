# GAMEPLAY APPROACHES

## Granny: Classic-Escape — Five Days to Get Out
**Core loop:** The player wakes locked inside Granny's house. The run is divided into DAYS, which double as LIVES: you start with 5 days. A day is one continuous play period that ends one of two ways — Granny catches you (knockout, lose a day, respawn in a fixed bedroom with progress on locks/items PRESERVED), or you open the main exit and escape (win). There is no per-day countdown clock; the only pressure source is Granny herself, so pacing is emergent from her hunt.

THE 8–12 SECOND MICRO-LOOP the player actually lives in:
1) STOP & LISTEN (1–3s). The player holds still in cover and reads AUDIO. This is the PRIMARY sense — you hear Granny far more often than you see her. Procedural footstep impulses fire every ~0.55s when she walks, ~0.32s when alerted. Each is spatialized via a Web Audio PannerNode, attenuated by a 1/d distance falloff clamped to [1m..8m], and passed through a low-pass BiquadFilter whose cutoff drops from 8000Hz (same room, door open) to ~600Hz (through a wall) so the muffling tells you whether a wall is between you. The grandfather clock ticks at 1Hz as a constant ambient anchor.
2) CHOOSE MOVEMENT MODE (explicit numbers): Crouch-sneak = 1.2 m/s, noise radius 1.5m, camera/capsule height 0.9m, near silent. Walk = 2.6 m/s, noise radius 5m, height 1.65m. Sprint = 5.2 m/s, noise radius 11m, height 1.65m; stamina 100→0 over 6s, regen 0→100 over 9s, re-sprint locked until stamina>25. Standing still emits ZERO noise events. Every footstep while moving emits one discrete NOISE EVENT {position=feet, loudness=mode radius}.
3) ACT ON THE WORLD. Interact ray from camera, 2.5m reach, target must be within reach AND have clear line-of-sight. Pick up an item, open a drawer (prismatic constraint) or door (hinge constraint, real swing), or use an item at its lock. Careless acts spike noise: slam a door = radius 14m; drop a heavy item = 12m; hard furniture bump (impact impulse > 4 N·s) scales up to 10m; easing a door open slowly (hold interact) = 3m.
4) READ THREAT & RESET. Check the AWARENESS METER (0–100, top of screen). If Granny is hunting, either break line-of-sight and go silent to bleed her awareness down, or commit to a hide spot (under bed / in wardrobe; enter takes 0.8s and emits a 2m noise). Awareness decays −20/s once she has no sound or sight of you for 2s. When safe, return to step 1 and advance one rung of the puzzle chain.

The loop is: LISTEN → PICK A LANE → ADVANCE THE PUZZLE A LITTLE → RETREAT/RESET. Progress is made in small, interruptible increments; you almost never finish a sub-goal in one uninterrupted trip.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Open the main exit (heavy front door with 3 distinct locks) and step through the porch threshold trigger to win.
- Locate and collect a chain of hidden items, each gating the next (see itemChain).
- Survive: do not get caught more than 4 times (the 5th catch = game over).
- Map the house: 2 floors + basement, ~11 rooms; learn item spawn zones and Granny's patrol nodes.
- Manage noise: perform loud actions (shouldering doors, drilling vents, cutting padlocks, dropping the battery) only when Granny is confirmed far away by ear.
- Optional/secondary: barricade doors with pushable furniture to buy time; find the shotgun + shell as a one-time 18s Granny-stun for the final exit dash.

**Item chain:**
- START: Player has empty hands in the locked upstairs bedroom (its door is jammed, not locked — shoulder it open, emits 6m noise).
- RUNG 1 — RUSTY KEY (kitchen drawer OR sideboard, 1 of 3 deterministic per-seed spots) -> unlocks the BASEMENT DOOR, which holds the heaviest tools and the alarm fuse box.
- RUNG 2 — SCREWDRIVER (basement workbench or garage shelf) -> unscrews the HALLWAY VENT COVER, revealing a cavity containing the SAFE CODE NOTE (a 4-digit code on a procedurally-textured paper scrap).
- RUNG 3 — SAFE CODE NOTE -> opens the WALL SAFE behind a painting in the study. Safe contains MAIN-DOOR LOCK #1 KEY (brass key) and the CUTTER HANDLE/COG.
- RUNG 4 — BOLT CUTTER (assembled from cutter body in basement + handle/cog from the safe) -> cuts the PADLOCK = MAIN-DOOR LOCK #2.
- RUNG 5 — CAR BATTERY (heavy, garage; must be physically carried, slows player to 1.8 m/s and forces walk-noise). Placing it on the GARAGE WINCH is a rubble-blocked RED HERRING; placed instead on the HALLWAY POWER PANEL it re-enables the electric keypad for MAIN-DOOR LOCK #3.
- RUNG 6 — KEYPAD CODE hidden as fridge-magnet digits (procedural) -> entering it disarms MAIN-DOOR LOCK #3 (electronic deadbolt).
- FINAL — With Lock #1 (brass key), Lock #2 (padlock cut), and Lock #3 (keypad) cleared, the MAIN DOOR opens, arming a 10-second ESCAPE ALARM that sets Granny to max Hunt; player must cross the porch threshold within 10s. Optional shotgun stun buys a clean exit if she is already close.
- WIN = cross the porch threshold trigger. Every rung's item, lock, and code spot is chosen deterministically per-seed at world-gen and collision-validated so nothing floats or clips.

**Tension:**
- Sound-first detection: you spend most of the game blind to Granny's exact position, inferring it from muffled footsteps — uncertainty IS the tension. The low-pass-through-walls cue makes every creak a question.
- Noise budget on every action: the chain REQUIRES loud actions, so progress and safety are in direct conflict; you must earn quiet windows before spending them.
- Awareness meter as a slow-burn alarm: SIGHT fills it fast (90° FOV cone, 12m range, LOS raycast; +35/s centered+lit, +15/s peripheral). At 100 she enters HUNT and sprints your last-known position. It is a dread bar, not instant death.
- Patrol / Search / Hunt states: Patrol (1.4 m/s, fixed node loop), Search (investigates a noise event ~12s, sweeping), Hunt (5.4 m/s straight to last-known then search). Escalation is audible (breathing pitch rises), giving a readable threat ladder.
- Hide-spot trap: wardrobes/under-bed make you SAFE but BLIND and committed — Granny opens the wardrobe (3s peek) if her awareness>60 when she arrives, so hiding too early or too obviously is a gamble.
- Persistent progress across lives: a catch costs a DAY but not your cleared locks/items, so the meta-arc is 'escape in as few days as possible' — defeat is a setback, not a wipe, which keeps players pushing.
- Final-door alarm spike: the win condition ends the quiet game with a 10s panic sprint, converting accumulated map knowledge into one cathartic dash — tension peaks exactly at release.
- Furniture barricades: shoving a dresser against a door buys ~6–9s of Granny pushing it (hinge torque vs body mass); a player-authored tension valve that rewards spatial planning but reveals your position via noise.

**Fun factors:**
- Mastery of the house: replays turn a terrifying maze into a known instrument — you learn creaky floorboards (loud tiles emit a 4m noise above walk speed), sightline corners, and the fastest lock order. The fun is the gap between Day-1 panic and Day-5 fluency.
- Diegetic, earned information: every clue (codes on paper, fridge magnets, muffled-footstep direction) is read from the world, not a HUD marker, so solving feels like real deduction.
- Self-authored risk/reward: drill the vent now while she's two rooms away, or wait one more patrol cycle? The game never decides for you — it just makes you pay for guessing wrong.
- Physics horror/comedy: pushing a dresser, a door swinging and slamming on its hinge, dropping the car battery mid-escape — emergent tactile moments that land because they are real rigid bodies.
- Close-call catharsis: the under-bed hide while Granny's feet shuffle past at 0.6m with awareness ticking down is the signature held-my-breath beat the genre lives for.
- Seeded variety: item/lock/code spots reshuffle per seed within 3 validated candidate slots each, so the logical puzzle is constant but the treasure hunt changes, supporting repeat runs and speed-route optimization.
- A designed ending: stepping through the door after a 10-second alarm sprint with Granny's hand swiping behind you is a real payoff, not an anticlimactic fade.

**Session:** First-ever run (learning the house, dying a few times): 25–40 minutes across all 5 days. A knowledgeable player typically escapes on Day 2–3 in ~12–18 minutes. An optimized speed-route (known seed, perfect lock order, no detection) is 4–7 minutes for a single clean day. A single day/life before a catch averages 3–6 minutes of active play. Target: a satisfying full arc fits in one sub-40-minute sitting, with strong 'one more run, fewer days' pull-back.
**Curve:** Day 1 (Lives 5–4) — TUTORIAL BY FEAR: Granny patrols slowly (1.2 m/s), Search lasts 8s, Hunt 4.6 m/s, wardrobe-peek disabled, generous awareness decay (−25/s). Player learns controls, noise modes, and the first 1–2 rungs (rusty key, basement). Day 2–3 (Lives 4–2) — RAMP: Patrol 1.4 m/s, Search 12s with wider sweep, Hunt 5.4 m/s, wardrobe-peek enabled at awareness>60, Granny re-checks spots where she last heard you. The middle rungs (screwdriver, safe, bolt cutter, battery) demand loud actions and real planning. Day 4 (Life 2→1) — PRESSURE: Granny adds a random-investigate impulse every 45s toward a noisy zone, fully exploits the player's own loud-action audio, awareness decay drops to −18/s. Day 5 (Last life) — CLUTCH: Patrol 1.6 m/s, Hunt 5.8 m/s; she pre-emptively guards the main door if Locks #1–#2 are already cleared (she 'knows' you're close), making the final keypad + alarm sequence the hardest moment. Difficulty scales with BOTH days elapsed AND puzzle progress, so the climax is always tense regardless of player skill.

**Failure modes:**
- BORING if Granny is too predictable: a pure fixed-node patrol becomes a memorized conga line. FIX: noise-driven Search detours plus a periodic random-investigate impulse (Day 4+); never visibly telegraph her next node.
- BORING if detection is binary/instant: instant-catch-on-sight removes the slow-dread payoff and punishes exploration. FIX: gradual awareness fill+decay and the Search-before-Hunt ladder, so getting spotted is usually recoverable.
- UNFAIR if audio localization is unreadable: wrong muffling/panning collapses the sound-first design into guesswork. FIX: validate the 1/d falloff and low-pass-through-walls cutoff by ear; make same-room vs through-wall unmistakable; keep the always-on clock tick as a calibration anchor.
- UNFAIR if the item chain is ambiguous: a player who can't deduce the next rung wanders and blames the game. FIX: each item gives a one-line diegetic hint ('this key looks like a basement lock'); codes sit near their target (magnets by the keypad); all 3 candidate spots per item are in thematically obvious rooms.
- CHEESEABLE if a corner lets you wait Granny out forever: a single safe tile trivializes the game. FIX: random-investigate can route her anywhere; standing still >40s with progress incomplete triggers a house 'creak' noise event nudging her toward your wing.
- CHEESEABLE if barricades are infinitely strong: a permanently sealed door breaks the chase. FIX: escalating shove torque yields any single-furniture barricade in 6–9s, heavier stacks add only a few seconds, and shoving emits loud noise that reveals her — a trade, not an exploit.
- FRUSTRATING if persistent progress is unclear: players who think a catch wipes unlocks will quit. FIX: on respawn show 'Day 3 of 5 — Locks 1/3, Items: key, screwdriver' so meta-progress and stakes are explicit.
- FRUSTRATING if the final alarm is unwinnable from the wrong spot: arming a 10s alarm with Granny between you and the threshold is a guaranteed unfair loss. FIX: the alarm summons her from her CURRENT position, the porch threshold is <8m from the door, and the optional shotgun stun is an explicit insurance item for players who route for it.
- ANTICLIMACTIC if escape just fades out. FIX: scripted final-second hand-swipe near-miss, door slam behind you, then silence and a 'Escaped on Day N — caught X times' results screen that invites a lower-day retry.

**Win/Lose:** WIN: Clear all three main-door locks (RUNG-1 brass key, RUNG-4 padlock cut, RUNG-6 keypad code), open the main door (which arms a 10-second escape alarm and sets Granny to max Hunt), and cross the porch threshold trigger volume before the timer expires. Crossing = immediate win; results screen shows day count and number of times caught. LOSE (per day): Granny reaches melee range (within 1.2m with awareness>0) and lands her catch swing -> screen-fade knockout, day counter increments toward 5, player respawns in the start bedroom with ALL collected items and cleared locks preserved; Granny resets to a patrol node away from the player. GAME OVER: the 5th catch (last day spent) -> no respawn, 'Granny got you' end screen, run resets to a new seed. There is no time-of-day clock failure state — the run ends only by escaping (win) or exhausting all 5 days (lose).

## Granny: Pressure-Cooker
**Core loop:** The loop runs on a hard SESSION TIMER of 8 real minutes (480 s), the spine of the whole design. The player wakes locked in a 2-storey house (~14m x 11m footprint, 9 rooms + basement) and must assemble and execute an escape before the timer hits zero OR before Granny kills them 3 times.

MOMENT-TO-MOMENT (the 4-8 second decision cycle the player repeats ~80-120 times per session):
1) STAND AT A THRESHOLD. The player is in a doorway or behind cover. They check three readable signals: (a) the dynamic-music layer (silent = safe, low cello drone = Granny in adjacent room, fast staccato = Granny has a SIGHT lock, see tensionMechanics), (b) the on-floor footstep audio with stereo+distance falloff (procedural wood-creak impulse, -6dB per ~3m), (c) the awareness meter ring around the crosshair (0-100%).
2) CHOOSE A GAIT. Three movement states with explicit numbers:
   - Crouch-sneak: 1.1 m/s, noise radius 1.5 m, near-silent footsteps (one creak per 1.4 s, 18% chance to emit a noise event).
   - Walk: 2.6 m/s, noise radius 4.5 m, footstep noise event every 0.5 s.
   - Sprint: 5.0 m/s, noise radius 9.0 m, footstep noise event every 0.28 s, PLUS a continuous "running" noise tag Granny can track even without line-of-sight.
   The risk/reward is literal and constant: sprinting crosses a 6m room in 1.2 s vs 5.5 s crouched, but emits a noise event Granny hears from 9 m through walls. This is THE decision the player makes every few seconds.
3) ACT ON AN OBJECT. Pick up an item (E, 0.4 s animation, silent), open a drawer/cabinet (0.6 s, emits a 3 m noise event — searching is audible), open a door (push physics, see below), or solve an in-place puzzle (lockpick / code entry, 4-9 s of being STUCK in place and vulnerable).
4) MANAGE THE BODY. Player has STAMINA (100, drains 14/s sprinting, regens 9/s when not sprinting, can't sprint below 8). And a HEARTBEAT meter that rises when Granny is within 6 m (audible procedural thump that itself can mask Granny's footsteps — a deliberate sensory-overload mechanic).
5) DROP THINGS = NOISE SPIKE. Carrying an item, if the player sprints into furniture (collision impulse > 3 m/s) they fumble and DROP it: the item hits the floor as a rigid body and emits a 7 m noise event. This punishes panic-sprinting while carrying the escape items.

Every cycle is: read the threat -> price the speed-vs-noise tradeoff -> commit -> re-read. Granny's state machine (Patrol -> Investigate -> Chase -> Search -> Patrol) reacts to the noise/sight events the player generates, so the player is co-authoring the threat.

DOOR PHYSICS AS A LOOP VERB: doors are hinge-constrained rigid bodies. Opening normally is a 1.2 m noise event. SLAMMING a door behind you (Shift+drop) is a deliberate 8 m noise spike that buys ~3 s of Granny pathing delay if she's on the far side — a real tool, not decoration. The player can also PUSH furniture (chairs ~6 kg, dressers ~40 kg, requiring the player to walk into it; push force capped so a 40 kg dresser takes ~2.5 s of shoving) to BARRICADE a door: a barricaded door costs Granny 4 s to force, during which her loud forcing animation tells the player exactly where she is.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Escape the house before the 8:00 timer expires and with fewer than 3 deaths. There are 3 escape routes, each a separate item->lock chain (see itemChain): the FRONT DOOR (4 locks, the 'intended'/hardest route), the BASEMENT CELLAR HATCH (2 locks + a generator step, medium), and the MASTER-BEDROOM WINDOW (1 plank-pry + 1 fuse, fastest but most exposed — upstairs, far from cover).
- Find your hidden CAR KEYS only matter on Hard+ where the front door opens to a garage instead of the street — fold this in as the escalation, not the base objective.
- MICRO-OBJECTIVE that structures every session: locate the 4 hidden items needed for your CHOSEN route. Items spawn deterministically per-seed in collision-validated rest spots (on shelves, in drawers, under the bed) — never floating, never inside walls.
- SOFT OBJECTIVE / scoring: escape FAST and QUIET. A run that escapes at 6:30 with 40 total noise events scores higher than one at 7:55 with 200. This drives replay and the risk/reward identity even though it is not pass/fail.
- SURVIVAL sub-goal each time Granny chases: break line-of-sight and stay un-spotted for 8 s to send her from Chase back to Search, then to Patrol. Hiding spots (under 2 beds, inside 3 wardrobes) are the tool; they are NOT infinite — see failureModes.

**Item chain:**
- FRONT DOOR ROUTE (hardest, 4 links): RUSTY KEY (in a kitchen drawer, opens the PADLOCK on the front-door deadbolt cage) -> SCREWDRIVER (on the garage workbench, unscrews the metal PLATE bolted over the second lock) -> 3-DIGIT CODE: the code is not an item but a deduction — three numbered notes (bedroom, bathroom, living room) each give one digit + position, entered at the door keypad in a 6 s vulnerable stand-still -> MASTER LOCK BAR which only lifts once the other 3 are cleared. Door then swings on its hinge constraint and the player runs out = WIN.
- BASEMENT CELLAR ROUTE (medium, 3 links): FUSE (in the living-room clock) -> insert into BASEMENT GENERATOR and pull the start cord (loud 10 m noise spike, 3 s animation — a deliberate big-risk moment) which powers the CELLAR HATCH winch -> WINCH CRANK HANDLE (hidden under basement stairs) cranks the hatch open over 4 s = WIN.
- WINDOW ROUTE (fastest, 2 links): CROWBAR (behind the furnace in basement — forces the player DOWN into Granny's favored patrol zone, the risk tax for the fast route) -> pry the BOARDED master-bedroom window (3 pry actions, each a 5 m noise event) = WIN. Only viable if the player accepts being loud and exposed upstairs.
- CROSS-ROUTE GATING: the SCREWDRIVER and CROWBAR are the only tools that can also pry open 2 jammed supply cabinets containing a MEDKIT (removes 1 death from your count) and a NOISE-DAMP RAG (wrap shoes, -40% footstep noise for 90 s) — so tool choice ripples into survivability, not just door-opening.
- ANTI-SOFTLOCK RULE the implementer MUST enforce: at least one COMPLETE chain must always be solvable from the current world state. If the player drops the rusty key through a gap (shouldn't happen with collision-validated rest, but defensively), the item is teleported back to its last valid rest spot after 20 s out of reach. Validate every spawn with a downward raycast + an overlap test against static colliders before the level starts.

**Tension:**
- ESCALATION ON A SCHEDULE (the 'pressure cooker'). Five timed escalation beats, all keyed to the 480 s timer so dread ramps even if Granny never sees you: T+0:00 Granny base speed 2.3 m/s, hearing radius x1.0, house lights ON. T+2:00 Granny speed -> 2.7 m/s, hearing x1.2. T+4:00 LIGHTS FAIL (procedural flicker then blackout; player now depends on the flashlight, which itself is a directional giveaway). T+5:30 Granny speed -> 3.1 m/s, hearing x1.4, and her Search state lasts 50% longer (harder to shake). T+7:00 SECONDARY THREAT activates: a CROW/RAVEN nest or a roaming 'Spider' (procedural) that patrols a random corridor and, if touched, triggers a 6 m noise event AND a 1.5 s player stun — a chaos agent that punishes the now-frantic endgame movement.
- SIGHT DETECTION (fills tension fast). Granny vision cone: 70 deg total FOV, 12 m range, eye height 1.45 m (she's hunched). A line-of-sight raycast from her eye to the player's chest+head must be UNOBSTRUCTED by static geometry for detection — walls genuinely block sight. When the player is inside the cone AND visible, the AWARENESS meter fills at a rate scaled by distance and player gait: 40%/s at 4 m standing, 18%/s crouched, halved again if the player is in shadow (lit-state sampled from nearest light). At 100% Granny enters CHASE and the music slams to the staccato layer.
- HEARING DETECTION (the core risk/reward currency). Every noise event = {position, loudness_radius, timestamp}. If the event's radius reaches Granny's ears (distance < radius x current_hearing_multiplier), she enters INVESTIGATE and paths to the event's position (not the player's current position — so a noise the player made 3 s ago sends her to where they WERE, enabling misdirection). Walls do NOT block sound (realistic and tense) but distance falloff does. THROWN ITEMS as a tool: the player can throw a held item (right-click, 8 m/s) to create a noise event AWAY from themselves — the single most satisfying tension-release verb.
- DYNAMIC MUSIC as the tension HUD (procedural, Web Audio). Four crossfaded layers driven by Granny's state + proximity: Layer 0 ambient room-tone drone (always, ~55 Hz + filtered noise). Layer 1 a slow detuned cello-ish swell fades in as Granny enters the same/adjacent room (proximity < 8 m). Layer 2 a pulsing low synth that speeds its pulse rate with awareness-meter fill (60 BPM at 0% -> 150 BPM at 90%). Layer 3 chase staccato strings + a sub-bass hit, snapped on at CHASE start, tailed off over 2 s when she loses you. The music TELLS the player the threat level so dread is information, not confusion.
- HEARTBEAT FEEDBACK LOOP. Within 6 m of Granny the player hears their own procedural heartbeat (rate 70 -> 130 BPM as distance closes), AND the screen vignette pulses + slight FOV breathing. Crucially the heartbeat partially MASKS Granny's footstep audio at <3 m — so getting close enough to need the info is exactly when the info gets noisy. This manufactures the held-breath, 'is she past me yet?' moment.
- RELEASE VALVES (tension must be RELEASED or it becomes noise-fatigue). Three explicit ones: (1) Hiding under a bed / in a wardrobe drops player noise to 0 and shrinks the hittable collider; if un-spotted 8 s Granny de-escalates, music exhales down a layer — a designed sigh of relief. (2) Successfully throwing an item to misdirect Granny and slipping past. (3) Each LOCK CLEARED triggers a short positive audio sting + the awareness ring flashes green — progress is felt. The rhythm is deliberately: squeeze (escalation/chase) -> release (hide/misdirect/progress) -> squeeze, roughly every 45-75 s.
- FLASHLIGHT RISK/REWARD (post-blackout). After T+4:00 the flashlight (a SpotLight, 25 deg cone, 14 m throw, real shadows) is the only way to see — but Granny's vision cone gets a +30% awareness fill rate when the player's flashlight beam is pointed within her FOV (she sees the light). So the player must flick it off (F) and move blind-but-quiet when she's near. Battery: 100, drains 1.2/s on, no recharge — forces rationing in the final 4 minutes.

**Fun factors:**
- The speed-vs-noise decision is FRACTAL: it's the whole game (which route, loud or quiet) and also every single step (sneak or sprint this corridor?). A game where the core verb is interesting every 4 seconds is a fun game. The player is never just holding W.
- Granny is a SYSTEM the player learns to play, not a scripted jumpscare dispenser. Because she chases noise-event POSITIONS and can be misdirected with thrown items, slamming doors, and slipping out of her search radius, mastery feels like outwitting a creature. The skill ceiling is reading her state machine through the audio/music HUD.
- DREAD FROM INFORMATION, NOT CONFUSION. The dynamic music + heartbeat + footstep stereo give the player JUST enough to feel her closing in without seeing her — the scariest horror is the anticipation. The 4:00 blackout and 7:00 secondary threat guarantee the back half escalates into genuine panic even for skilled players.
- MULTIPLE ROUTES create real strategy and replayability: a confident player rushes the loud-but-fast window route; a cautious player grinds the quiet front-door chain. Per-seed item placement + the soft fast/quiet score means 'beat it again, better' has teeth.
- PHYSICS IS A TOY. Pushing a dresser to barricade a door, slamming a door in Granny's face, hurling a teacup across the room to send her the wrong way, fumbling and dropping the key in a panic — these emergent, physical moments are where the funniest and tensest stories come from, and they're only possible because the physics are real (per the hard requirements).
- THE 8-MINUTE TIMER makes every session a tight, complete, replayable arc with a clean adrenaline payoff — no 40-minute slog, no save-scumming. 'One more run' is built into the length.
- THE ESCAPE ITSELF is a designed crescendo: the last lock clears, the door swings on its hinge, the staccato music is screaming, Granny is 4 m behind, and the player SPRINTS through into daylight — the single hardest, loudest, most cathartic 3 seconds in the game. Earning silence after 8 minutes of dread is the reward.

**Session:** "A single run is hard-capped at 8:00 (480 s) by the escape timer; a typical SUCCESSFUL escape lands at 5:30-7:30 once the player knows a route. First-ever runs almost always end in death (a death sends you back to the last safe corner with a death counter +1, not a full restart, so a session is one continuous 8-minute arc rather than repeated reloads). Realistic engaged-session length is 25-45 minutes = roughly 4-7 full runs as the player learns the house seed, experiments with routes, and chases a faster/quieter clear. Designed deliberately short so the dread stays sharp and 'one more run' is frictionless."
**Curve:** "WITHIN a run: difficulty rises monotonically via the 5 escalation beats (Granny faster + better hearing at 2:00/5:30, blackout at 4:00, secondary threat at 7:00). The first 2 minutes are a deliberately gentle 'learn the house, gather items in the light' grace period; the last 90 s are intentionally brutal. ACROSS runs: a per-run STARTING DIFFICULTY tier that auto-adjusts — Easy after 2 consecutive deaths (Granny base 2.0 m/s, hearing x0.85, 4 deaths allowed, 9:00 timer), Normal default (values above), Hard after a clean escape (Granny base 2.6 m/s, hearing x1.15, blackout moved to 3:00, garage/car-key complication added, 2 deaths allowed, 7:00 timer). The mastery arc: run 1 = overwhelmed and die, runs 2-3 = learn item locations and Granny's patrol, runs 4-5 = commit to a route and survive, runs 6+ = optimize for the fast/quiet score on Hard. Numbers are exposed as named constants in one config object so the curve can be tuned without touching logic."

**Failure modes:**
- HIDING SPOT CHEESE: if hiding under a bed is fully safe and infinite, the optimal play is hide -> wait out timer-pressure -> dart, which is boring and breaks tension. PREVENT: hiding is safe ONLY if Granny didn't SEE you enter (if she has a fresh sight-lock she walks to the spot and forces you out = 1 death). Add a 'fidget' meter while hidden that fills over 12 s and forces a noise event (cough/creak) — you can't camp forever. The timer also punishes camping.
- NOISE-EVENT SPAM / SENSORY FATIGUE: if everything makes noise and the music is always blaring, players go numb and tension flatlines. PREVENT: enforce the squeeze/release rhythm — Granny MUST fully de-escalate to Patrol with silent music between encounters; cap chase encounters to feel earned (don't let her instantly re-aggro within 3 s of losing you — add a 3 s post-chase 'cooldown' where she searches but can't re-lock). Tune base ambient to genuine near-silence so spikes land.
- AI PATHING JANK breaking immersion: Granny stuck on a chair, vibrating in a doorway, or warping through furniture instantly destroys the dread. PREVENT: navmesh built ONLY on the static floor plan (furniture excluded from navmesh but present as physics colliders); Granny does local steering/obstacle-avoidance against dynamic furniture; if she's stuck (velocity < 0.1 m/s for 1.5 s while pathing) she recomputes a path or, last resort, teleports to her last valid navmesh node OFF-SCREEN only (never in player view — check her position is outside the player's frustum + behind a wall first).
- SIGHT-RAYCAST FALSE POSITIVES: a single ray to the player's center clips a thin doorframe and Granny 'can't see' an obviously-visible player, or a ray through a 1px gap detects through a wall. PREVENT: cast 3 rays (head, chest, hips), require >=2 unobstructed for a sight hit; give the raycast a small radius/multiple sample points; ensure all static colliders are watertight (no sub-mm gaps between wall panels — weld or overlap them by 2 cm).
- PHYSICS TUNNELING / JITTER (explicitly forbidden by the client): fast Granny or a thrown item passes through a wall, or barricade furniture vibrates. PREVENT: use cannon-es with CCD-style substepping (fixed timestep 1/60, max 3 substeps), keep min collider thickness >= 0.1 m, cap all velocities at 12 m/s, set contactEquationStiffness high enough that the dresser doesn't sink, and sleep static-resting furniture to kill idle jitter.
- TIMER FEELS ARBITRARY / UNFAIR: a hard 8:00 cutoff with no in-world justification feels like a cheap loss. PREVENT: diegetic framing — the timer is 'dawn' / 'Granny's medication wearing off' shown as a slowly brightening window + an in-world wall clock; surface it as escalating audio (a distant church bell at each escalation beat) so the player FEELS the clock instead of staring at a number. The clock is the antagonist as much as Granny is.
- ROUTE IMBALANCE: if one escape route is strictly faster AND safer, the other two are dead content. PREVENT: enforce the risk triangle — window route is fastest but loudest/most exposed (upstairs, far from hides), front-door route is safest but longest (4 links, lots of searching = lots of drawer noise), basement route is medium but forces a single huge mandatory noise spike (the generator pull). Playtest the fast/quiet score so all three can post competitive clears.
- DARKNESS FRUSTRATION post-blackout: total blackness + a weak flashlight + a fast Granny can tip from tense to unfair/unreadable. PREVENT: keep a faint procedural moonlight ambient (never pure black, ~0.04 ambient term), keep Granny faintly rim-lit by a cold fill light so a spotted Granny is always legible at <6 m, and ensure the audio HUD (music + footsteps + heartbeat) fully conveys her position even when sight fails — the blackout should shift the player onto EARS, not blind them.

**Win/Lose:** "WIN: complete any one of the three item->lock->escape chains and physically move the player body through the opened exit (door swings on its hinge / hatch winches up / window pries open) before the 8:00 timer expires. On crossing the exit trigger volume, freeze the timer, kill the threat music, swell a resolution chord, fade to a results screen showing time, total noise events, deaths used, and a letter grade (S/A/B/C) from the fast+quiet score. WIN raises next-run difficulty one tier. LOSE (two ways): (1) DEATHS — Granny catches the player (her hand-collider overlaps the player capsule during Chase, or she forces a hiding spot she saw you enter); each catch = death +1, a procedural scream + 0.5 s screen-shake, and respawn at the last safe room corner with a 3 s invulnerability and Granny reset to Patrol across the map. Reaching the death cap (Normal=3) = game over. (2) TIME — the 8:00 dawn timer expires before any escape completes = game over (diegetic: Granny's full strength returns / it's morning and the doors auto-lock). LOSE drops next-run difficulty one tier after 2 consecutive losses. Both lose states go to the same results screen with a 'Try Again' that re-rolls nothing (same seed) so the player can apply what they learned, plus a 'New House' that re-seeds."

## Granny: The Long Night — Systemic-Sandbox Loop
**Core loop:** PREMISE: You wake locked in the house of a deranged old woman (Granny). You have 5 in-game days (each = one wake/sleep cycle, ~6 real minutes of active play per day, so a full win run is ~25-35 min) to assemble an escape. Granny patrols the house hunting you by SOUND and SIGHT. If she catches you she knocks you out; you lose 1 day and respawn in the locked starting bedroom. Run out of days = game over.

THE 8-12 SECOND MOMENT-TO-MOMENT LOOP (this is what the player actually does, repeated hundreds of times):
1) OBSERVE (1-3s): From cover, check Granny's position via audio cues (her footsteps are a procedural low-thud at 0.5s interval when walking, 0.25s when chasing) and her muttering (a positional Web Audio source so you can localize her by stereo pan + volume). Read her AWARENESS METER (a subtle vignette + heartbeat audio that intensifies as she nears detecting you). Decide: move, hide, or manipulate.
2) DECIDE A MOVEMENT MODE: Crouch-sneak (1.2 m/s, ~0.6m noise radius, near-silent), Walk (2.6 m/s, 4m noise radius), Sprint (5.0 m/s, 9m noise radius + footfall spikes). The core tension knob is SPEED vs SILENCE. Every meter you cross is a gamble on her hearing.
3) ACT ON THE WORLD (the systemic payload): pick up an item (hold E, 0.4s), throw an item to create a NOISE LURE at the impact point, push furniture to barricade a doorway, set a trap, open/close a door (closing slowly = silent, slamming = 8m noise spike), or interact with a lock/puzzle node (which takes 2-6s of vulnerable channel time).
4) RESOLVE: Either you advanced the escape chain (placed an item in its slot, broke a lock, blocked a route) or you triggered a state change in Granny (she heard the lure and is now walking toward a DECOY position, buying you 8-15s). 
5) REASSESS: New Granny position, new awareness level, repeat.

The genius of the systemic version: there is almost never one correct action. Need to cross the main hall but Granny's in it? You can (a) crouch the long way around, (b) throw a wine bottle into the kitchen to pull her there, (c) push the bookshelf to barricade the hall door behind you so she can't follow, (d) shut and lock a door on her, or (e) sprint and gamble. The physics makes all five real and all five have honest costs.

PHYSICS IS THE TOY: Because cannon-es bodies are real, the player discovers (not is told) emergent combos: stack two chairs under the upstairs window to reach it without finding the ladder; wedge a fallen wardrobe so it pins the basement door; knock a vase off a shelf as a remote-triggered noise by shooting it loose; barricade yourself in a room, then realize Granny can break a barricade over ~6s of loud bashing (which tells YOU exactly where she is and how long you have).

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Escape the house alive before Day 5 ends via ANY of three routes (Front Door, Basement Hatch, Upstairs Window). Each route has its own item->lock chain; you only need to complete ONE.
- Find and collect the route-specific KEY ITEMS scattered across the house (deterministically placed on real surfaces, collision-validated, never floating/clipping).
- Manipulate Granny's position using noise lures and line-of-sight breaks to create safe windows for the vulnerable channel-time actions (unlocking, assembling, climbing).
- Survive Granny encounters: break line of sight before the awareness meter fills, or barricade/trap to escape an active chase.
- MASTERY (implicit, replay): Win faster, win on fewer days, win on the hardest route (Front Door requires the longest chain), or win without ever being caught (perfect run).
- OPTIONAL self-set goals the sandbox enables: lure Granny into a bear trap, barricade her into a room, escape using only stacked furniture instead of the intended ladder.

**Item chain:**
- === ROUTE A: FRONT DOOR (hardest, longest chain — the 'intended' route) ===
- MAIN DOOR has 3 locks: a PADLOCK, a KEYPAD, and a deadbolt jammed with a BROKEN HANDLE.
- PADLOCK needs the MASTER KEY -> Master Key rests on a hook inside Granny's BEDROOM (high-risk room, she returns there to 'rest' ~every 90s for 12s).
- KEYPAD needs a 4-DIGIT CODE -> code is split: 2 digits painted on the back of a PHOTO FRAME in the living room (must pick it up + rotate to read), 2 digits scratched inside the BASEMENT fuse box (which is dark — requires FLASHLIGHT, found in the kitchen drawer).
- DEADBOLT needs a replacement HANDLE -> the WORKSHOP cabinet is locked by a COMBINATION PADLOCK whose combo is found on a sticky note under the upstairs BATHROOM sink. Inside the cabinet: the spare DOOR HANDLE + a SCREWDRIVER. Screwdriver also doubles as a weapon-of-opportunity / pry tool.
- === ROUTE B: BASEMENT HATCH (medium) ===
- BASEMENT cellar hatch is bolted with a PADLODK + blocked by a heavy WARDROBE you must physically PUSH aside (real physics, ~4s of shoving, loud).
- PADLOCK needs BOLT CUTTERS -> bolt cutters hang in the GARAGE, but the garage interior door is barricaded from Granny's side; you must pull the furniture away (loud) OR enter via the kitchen pass-through window by stacking a crate.
- Hatch also requires POWER to the cellar light to navigate the crawl tunnel — restore the FUSE (found in the same fuse box as the code) OR just use the FLASHLIGHT.
- === ROUTE C: UPSTAIRS WINDOW (fastest but most exposed) ===
- Upstairs HALL WINDOW is nailed shut and 1.5m above a sloped porch roof. Needs the HAMMER (claw end) to pry the boards -> Hammer is on the workbench in the WORKSHOP (same locked cabinet as Route A's handle — opening it serves two routes).
- Window also needs you to REACH it: find the LADDER (in the garage, same place as bolt cutters) OR emergently STACK two pushable chairs/crates under it (physics solution the player can discover).
- After prying: the drop to the porch roof must be survivable (a real physics drop of ~1.5m, no fall damage at that height) then a 2.5m drop to the lawn = ESCAPE.
- === SHARED / UNIVERSAL ITEMS ===
- FLASHLIGHT (kitchen drawer): unlocks dark areas for ALL routes, but its beam is a SIGHT-amplifier FOR Granny too (she spots the moving cone faster).
- CROWBAR (under the starting bed — your first findable item): pries one boarded door early, and is the all-purpose 'I'm stuck' fallback tool.
- NOISE OBJECTS (bottles, cans, plates — abundant, infinite-ish): throwable lures, the renewable resource that powers the lure subsystem.

**Tension:**
- AWARENESS METER (0-100): Sight raises it fast (+25/s in full FOV cone with LOS at <8m, +12/s at 8-15m). Sound raises it in steps (a noise event inside her hearing radius adds a one-shot spike: footstep-in-radius +8, slammed door +40, dropped object +25). Above 60 = SUSPICIOUS (she walks to last-known position). Above 100 = CHASE. Decays -10/s only when she has NO sight and NO recent noise for 2s. The slow decay is the tension: you don't instantly become safe, you have to EARN safety by hiding and waiting.
- HEARING SYSTEM (numeric, the heart of it): every player action emits a NoiseEvent{position, loudness} where loudness is a radius in meters. Granny does a distance check each event; if dist <= loudness she gains awareness AND sets her investigate-target to that position. Sprint emits continuous 9m events every 0.3s. Crouch emits 0.6m every 0.5s (effectively silent unless she's right next to you). This makes the speed/silence tradeoff a literal spatial-audio puzzle.
- SIGHT SYSTEM: vision cone of 90deg horizontal FOV, 15m range. Each frame (throttled to 10Hz for perf) she casts a ray from her eyes to the player; if within cone AND ray hits player before any wall collider, she sees you. Walls, closed doors, and pushed furniture ALL block the ray (the physics colliders double as occluders — barricades literally blind her).
- LURE / DECOY DYNAMIC (release valve): a thrown bottle creates a loud NoiseEvent at impact; Granny abandons her current target and investigates the louder/nearer event. This is the player's primary tension-RELEASE tool — you spend a renewable resource (junk) to buy 8-15s of safe passage. Skilled play = chaining lures to walk her around the house like a puppet.
- BARRICADE PRESSURE: you push furniture (real mass-based physics; a wardrobe is ~80kg, takes ~4s to shove into a doorway) to block a route. Granny BREAKS barricades by bashing (loud rhythmic thuds, ~6s for light furniture, ~12s for heavy). The bashing audio is both terrifying AND informative — it's a countdown timer you can hear and locate.
- THE 'REST' RHYTHM: Granny periodically returns to her bedroom to rock in her chair for 12s (telegraphed by a creaking-chair audio loop). This is a predictable safe-window the player learns to exploit for the riskiest actions (grabbing the Master Key from her room). Predictability here is GOOD — it rewards observation.
- PROXIMITY HEARTBEAT: a procedural heartbeat (Web Audio, kick-drum synth) whose BPM scales with Granny's distance (60 BPM at 12m, 140 BPM at 3m) and her awareness. Pure tension delivery with zero UI clutter; tells you 'she's close' without a minimap.
- DAY TIMER as macro-tension: each day she gets faster (chase speed +0.4 m/s/day) and her hearing radius grows (+10%/day). The walls close in across a run.

**Fun factors:**
- EXPRESSION: The same locked door has 5 honest solutions (sneak / lure / barricade-and-pass / lock-her-out / sprint-gamble). Players invent their own playstyle and the physics validates ALL of them. This is the core promise of the systemic angle.
- EMERGENT PHYSICS COMEDY+POWER: stacking chairs to reach a window, accidentally knocking a shelf of bottles down and pulling Granny right to you, wedging a wardrobe so perfectly she can never break through. The 'I can't believe that worked' moments are generated by the sim, not scripted — infinitely re-tellable, which is what makes horror-sandbox clips spread.
- PUPPETEERING GRANNY: once you understand the hearing system, luring her room-to-room with thrown bottles feels like outsmarting a predator. Mastery converts terror into control — the satisfying power-fantasy arc of the genre.
- LEGIBLE, LOCALIZABLE THREAT: because she's tracked by positional procedural audio (footsteps, muttering, chair-creak, barricade-bashing), the house becomes an audio-map. Skill = listening. This makes tension feel FAIR, not cheap-jumpscare random.
- MULTIPLE ROUTES = MULTIPLE GAMES: a Front-Door run feels like a heist (long chain, deep into her territory); a Window run feels like a frantic dash; a Basement run is a brute-force shove-fest. One house, three distinct experiences, strong replay.
- EARNED CATHARSIS: the final escape action (prying the last board, the door swinging open, the drop to the lawn) is a long vulnerable channel-time moment with Granny potentially bearing down — the highest-tension second in the game resolves into pure relief. The release IS the payoff.
- DISCOVERY OF SECRETS: split codes, hidden notes under sinks, digits on the back of a photo — the house rewards curiosity and observation, and that knowledge persists in the PLAYER across runs even after a respawn resets items.

**Session:** "A single 'attempt' (one day before catch-or-progress) is ~5-6 minutes. A full successful run is typically 25-35 minutes across the 5-day budget for a first-time winner; experienced players can complete the Window route in ~12-15 min and a flawless Front-Door run in ~20 min. The hard 5-day cap guarantees a session can't exceed ~45-50 min, so there's always a clean stopping/win point. Designed for one-more-day pickup play: each respawn is a fresh 5-6 min loop with knowledge carried forward."
**Curve:** "DAY 1 (TUTORIAL-BY-DESIGN, no hand-holding text): Granny is slow (chase 4.6 m/s vs player sprint 5.0 — you can outrun her in a straight line), hearing radii at baseline, she rests often (every 75s). The crowbar under the bed teaches pickup; the first boarded door teaches pry; an unavoidable bottle on a shelf teaches that noise = danger. Player learns the loop safely and likely scouts one route. DAY 2-3 (COMPETENCE): chase speed +0.4-0.8 m/s (now she keeps pace — straight-line sprinting no longer saves you, forcing use of LOS breaks and barricades), hearing +10-20%, rest interval grows to 90-105s. Player commits to a route and starts assembling its chain. DAY 4 (PRESSURE): chase speed ~5.4 m/s (faster than you — you MUST use the environment, can't outrun), hearing +30%, she patrols smarter (investigates last-known + adjacent room). The systemic tools become mandatory, not optional. DAY 5 (CLIMAX): chase 5.8 m/s, hearing +40%, minimal resting, she 'remembers' which route you've been working (biases patrol toward your active escape node). It's a do-or-die finale where all your accumulated route knowledge pays off. The curve is: outrun -> outmaneuver -> outsmart -> all-or-nothing. AUTO-SOFTENING: if the player is caught 2x on the same day, the next respawn grants a 10s grace period where Granny is forced to rest, preventing spawn-camping death spirals."

**Failure modes:**
- LURE EXPLOIT (most likely cheese): if a thrown bottle ALWAYS fully resets her to a decoy, players just spam bottles and walk past trivially. FIX: lures only redirect her if the new event is louder OR closer than her current target; during active CHASE (awareness 100) lures are 70% less effective (she's locked on); cap her 'investigate' commitment so a 3rd lure in 10s is ignored (she gets 'wise'). Tune so lures are powerful but not a press-to-win.
- BARRICADE TURTLING: player barricades the start room and waits out the timer. FIX: progress requires LEAVING (items are distributed; the day timer punishes stalling; and an un-progressed day is wasted toward game-over). Also Granny WILL break any barricade given time, so turtling is a delay not a win. Optionally: standing still too long with no progress triggers a creak that nudges her toward you.
- PHYSICS JANK KILLING IMMERSION (the client's stated rage): jitter, tunneling, items sinking through floors, furniture exploding. This is the #1 ship-killer. FIX: fixed physics timestep 1/60 with accumulator + interpolation; CCD enabled on the player capsule and all thrown/small dynamic bodies; collision margins ~0.04m; sleep thresholds so settled furniture stops simulating (no idle jitter); clamp max body velocity to ~20 m/s; never spawn bodies overlapping — validate placement with an AABB/ray test at load and nudge up until clear.
- FLOATING / CLIPPED ITEMS (client's #2 rage): items placed by hand-coded coordinates end up mid-air or inside a cabinet. FIX: deterministic placement = define surface anchor points (a named empty on each table/shelf), then raycast straight DOWN from slightly above the anchor onto the surface collider and rest the item's lowest AABB point on the hit point; reject any anchor whose downward ray also intersects another collider within the item's bounds. Snap, validate, log a console warning if any item fails validation at load.
- UNREACHABLE ITEM ON HIGH SHELF: an item placed where neither standing (eye ~1.6m, reach to ~2.1m) nor crouching can grab it. FIX: every item anchor must pass a reachability check — its grab point must be <=2.1m from floor on a walkable cell (player nav-validated), OR explicitly require a known tool (ladder/stacked furniture) and be flagged as such.
- GRANNY PATHING STUCK / T-POSE-WALKING-INTO-WALL: AI navmesh fails around player-pushed furniture (dynamic obstacles). FIX: use a coarse grid navmesh re-rasterized when large furniture moves (debounced ~0.5s); if path fails, Granny falls back to 'bash nearest blocking body' behavior (which is also a feature). Steering with raycast whisker avoidance for small dynamic clutter.
- ROUTE IMBALANCE: if the Window route is far easier, no one does the others. FIX: tune so each route has comparable expected time-cost: Window = fewest items but most exposed (high catch risk), Front Door = most items but uses the safe rest-rhythm, Basement = brute physics + loud. Playtest the win-rate per route to within ~10% of each other.
- AUDIO-AS-ONLY-SIGNAL FAILS FOR HEARING-IMPAIRED / BAD SPEAKERS: localizing Granny purely by sound excludes players and breaks on laptop speakers. FIX: provide an optional subtle directional UI indicator (edge-of-screen pulse toward Granny when she's <8m) and a 'last heard' marker, toggleable. Don't make the sole threat channel audio-only.
- TENSION COLLAPSE FROM OVER-MASTERY: once a player fully reads the AI, it stops being scary. FIX: the per-day difficulty ramp + the Day-5 'she remembers your route' behavior keep raising the floor; add small stochastic variation to her patrol so she's never 100% predictable; rare 'she fakes resting' beat on Day 4-5.
- RESPAWN PROGRESS CONFUSION: if collected items reset on catch, players feel robbed; if they don't, the game is trivial. FIX (chosen design): KEY-ITEMS placed into their slots STAY placed (your progress on the door persists), but loose items in your hand drop where you were caught (you may have to re-fetch). Knowledge always persists. This keeps catches costly but not soul-crushing.

**Win/Lose:** "WIN: Complete any ONE of the three escape chains and perform the final escape action — (A) all 3 front-door locks cleared + open door + step through threshold; (B) basement hatch unlocked + wardrobe shoved clear + descend the crawl tunnel to the exterior; (C) window boards pried + reach the sill (via ladder or stacked physics) + drop to porch roof + drop to lawn. Crossing the final exterior collider triggers the win state (escape sequence: door/window slams behind, Granny's scream fades, fade to dawn, run stats shown: days used, times caught, route taken, total time). LOSE: Day 5 ends (sleep cycle completes) without escaping = GAME OVER (she 'keeps you forever'). CATCH (not a loss, a setback): Granny reaches within 1.2m of the player during CHASE for >0.4s = knockout; lose the remaining current day, drop held loose items at catch location, respawn in locked starting bedroom at the next day's dawn; placed key-progress persists. Being caught on Day 5 with no days left = immediate GAME OVER. EDGE RULES: falling out of the world / clipping = soft-respawn at last grounded position (no day lost); if a route becomes physically impossible due to player error (e.g., wardrobe shoved irretrievably), the other two routes guarantee the game is always winnable, and a per-day item-reset on respawn restores any displaced non-key clutter."
